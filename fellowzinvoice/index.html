FellowzInvoice Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 October 2026

FellowzInvoice collects no personal data. It runs entirely on your own computer and makes no network connections.

What the app stores

Everything you type into FellowzInvoice — business details, client details, invoices, and your display settings — is saved locally on your device. It is never transmitted to us or to any third party, because the app has no server to send it to.

What we collect

  • No names, email addresses, or phone numbers
  • No location data
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No crash reports

Network activity

The app makes no network connections. It works fully offline, including on a computer with no internet connection at all.

Your control over your data

Your data stays in the app's local storage on your device. Deleting the app's data removes it permanently. We cannot access, recover, or restore it, because we never receive a copy.

Purchases

FellowzInvoice is sold through the Microsoft Store. Microsoft processes the payment and is the party that handles your billing information. Microsoft's own privacy statement governs that transaction; we never see your payment details.

Children

The app is a general-purpose business tool and is not directed at children. It collects nothing from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the date at the top of this page will change with it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: support@fellowz.app
