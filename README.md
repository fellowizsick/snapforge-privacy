# Privacy Policy — SnapForge

_Last updated: September 8, 2026_

SnapForge is a local screen capture and recording utility. This policy explains
what data it does and does not handle.

## Summary
SnapForge is **local-only.** It captures and records what you show on your own
screen, and everything stays on your computer. It does **not** upload, transmit,
or collect your captures, recordings, or personal data to any server we control
or any third party.

## What SnapForge handles
- **Screen captures and recordings** — produced by you, saved to your own
  Pictures\Screenshots and Videos folders (or the folders you choose). These are
  yours and never leave your device.
- **Audio** — if you enable mic/system audio recording, the recorded audio is
  mixed into the video file on your own machine. It is not uploaded anywhere.
- **Temporary recording files** — deleted automatically when a recording stops.

## Optional "Send to phone" feature
When you use the Send-to-Phone feature, SnapForge starts a **temporary HTTP
server on your local Wi-Fi network.** It serves a page that lets a device on the
same network browse and download your captures. This server:
- is bound to your local network only and is **not reachable from the internet;**
- **does not require any account, login, or signup;**
- runs only while the Send window is open and stops when you close it;
- transmits captures only to devices you scan the QR code with.

## What SnapForge does NOT do
- Does **not** collect analytics, crash reports, or usage telemetry.
- Does **not** sign you in to any account or collect identifiers.
- Does **not** show third-party ads.
- Does **not** read, upload, or share your files, captures, recordings, contacts,
  or browsing data.

## Permissions
SnapForge uses features native to your device:
- **Screen capture / recording** — the OS-level screen-recording permission in
  Windows.
- **Microphone / system audio** — only if you enable those toggles in the app.
- **Global hotkeys** — to let you snap/record from anywhere; it only triggers
  capture when you press the configured key.

You can revoke any of these at any time by turning off the relevant toggle,
uninstalling the app, or using the OS-level privacy setting.

## Data retention
Captures and recordings are kept only on your device in the folders you save
them to. You can delete them at any time. SnapForge keeps no cloud copy.

## Children's privacy
SnapForge does not collect personal information from anyone, including children,
because it does not collect personal information at all.
